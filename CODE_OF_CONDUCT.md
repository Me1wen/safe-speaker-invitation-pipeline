# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors must communicate respectfully, give constructive technical feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Project-Specific Expectations

- Use fictional data in public discussions and tests.
- Do not publish recipient lists, private contact information, credentials, or unredacted delivery logs.
- Do not propose features intended for deceptive, indiscriminate, or noncompliant outreach.
- Treat website operators, potential speakers, maintainers, and contributors with respect.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
